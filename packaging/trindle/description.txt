Text keeps your text snippets in one list you can search: a short trigger such as :addr and the words it stands for. You add, change and remove snippets with a simple form rather than editing YAML files, and every change keeps a backup first. Snippets that use variables, forms or scripts are shown exactly as they are and never rewritten. Text reads and writes Espanso's snippet files, so you can bring snippets in from Espanso on another computer and take them back out again (this needs your Documents and Downloads folders). On Trindle today you find a snippet and copy it to paste where you need it: typing a trigger and having it expand in other apps is not available in Trindle yet.
