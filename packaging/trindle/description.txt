Snippets keeps your text snippets in one list you can search: a short trigger such as addr# and the words it stands for. You add, change and remove snippets with a simple form rather than editing YAML files, and every change keeps a backup first. Snippets that use variables, forms or scripts are shown exactly as they are and never rewritten. Snippets reads and writes Espanso's snippet files, so you can bring snippets in from Espanso on another computer and take them back out again (this needs your Documents and Downloads folders). Once you allow Snippets to expand your short codes in its permissions, Trindle types the words for you when you type a trigger such as tn# in any program: Snippets itself never sees what you type. Snippets with variables, forms or scripts can be found and copied, but do not expand.
